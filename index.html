Support

Fairway Lab helps you estimate a practice putting line and record club distances for multiple golfers.

Putting

choose green speed and roll-past pace, enter distance and slopes, and read the estimated line. For multiple contours, add sections in ball-to-hole order. Slopes are percentages, not degrees.

Slope measurement

place the phone screen-up with its top edge toward the hole. One reading measures both uphill/downhill and side slope. Calibrate on a known level surface with the same phone case. Uneven grass and rocking affect results. Manual slope input is always available.

Club distances

select a golfer or add one from the golfer menu. Tap Add club to select a club. Tap a number to type a distance in yards. Use Carry/Total to switch metrics and the sliders button to choose up to three partial-swing columns; Full is always shown. Swipe a club left to delete it. The chart button opens historical Tour carry references.

Saved data

profiles, distances, settings and calibration are saved locally, without an account. Delete App in iOS Settings removes local app data; Offload App preserves it. Apple backups may retain a copy.

If a reading looks wrong, confirm phone orientation, calibrate on a known level surface, and check the slope against a trusted level. Estimates are for practice and are not guaranteed putting lines.
