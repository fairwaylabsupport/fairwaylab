Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Your information stays on your device

Fairway Lab does not collect or transmit your golfer names, club distances, putting inputs, calibration reference, or device-motion readings to the developer. The app has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or developer-operated servers.

Information saved locally

The app saves the golfer names and club distances you enter, selected columns, carry/total preference, putting settings, last-opened tab, and slope calibration using local iOS app storage. Live motion readings are processed on your device to estimate slope; the calibration reference and confirmed slope values are saved locally. Raw motion readings are not saved as a history.

Permissions

Motion sensors are used when you open slope measurement or calibration. Manual slope entry remains available. The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit club distances and remove golfer profiles in Club Distances. At least one profile remains; its clubs can be removed individually. Calibration can be reset from the measurement screen. To remove all locally stored app data, use Delete App in iOS Settings; Offload App preserves data. Device backups may retain copies according to your Apple settings. The app does not provide its own cloud sync or backup service.

External links and support

Opening a source or support link leaves the app for another service, whose privacy practices apply. If you contact support, your email provider and the support recipient receive the information you choose to send. Avoid including sensitive information. Support correspondence is used to respond to your request and resolve the issue, and retained only as needed for that purpose or legal obligations. For privacy or support questions, email fairwaylabsupport@gmail.com.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store transactions, diagnostics, and device backups under Apple's privacy policies and your settings. The developer does not receive your saved golfer profiles or motion readings through this app.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The current policy is available in the app and at its App Store privacy-policy link.
